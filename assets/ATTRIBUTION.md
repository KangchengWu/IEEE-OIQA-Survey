[← Back to the resource hub](../README.md)

The files `figures/gap.png`, `figures/distortion.png`, `figures/projection.png`, and `figures/methods.png` are the supplied research figures corresponding to Figures 1–4 of:

> Kangcheng Wu, Jiebin Yan, Hanwei Zhu, Jingwen Hou, and Yuming Fang. *A Survey of Omnidirectional Image Quality Assessment: Challenges, Status, and Future Work.*

Image processing is limited to compositing transparent pixels against white and saving lossless PNG files. Scientific content and figure text are preserved. Attribution and rights remain with the original authors; this package does not establish additional publication or reuse permissions.

`cover.png` is a geometric wireframe cover created for this resource hub. It contains no third-party icons, photographs, or brand marks.

Linked datasets, implementations, and papers retain their respective licenses and access conditions. Preserve the existing repository LICENSE when integrating this package.
